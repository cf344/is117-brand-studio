About this study

Reference: Drawing, Edward McKnight Kauffer, 1934, England. V&A, museum no. E.3770-2004. Image and caption inspected in the learner-supplied screenshot.

The drawing assembles a recognizable figure from repeated rectangles and circles. Here, rectangular content regions, consistent alignment and strong typographic contrast build a predictable reading path. The aim is to make choosing feel straightforward. Risk: the systematic layout could feel impersonal.
