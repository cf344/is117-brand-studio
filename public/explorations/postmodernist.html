About this study

Reference: Cabinet de Curiosité, Shiro Kuramata, 1988, Japan. V&A. Image and caption inspected in the learner-supplied screenshot.

The cabinet overlaps transparent frames and interrupted colored edges. Here, a displaced open frame and contrasting edge colors suggest layered structures; editorial serif lettering meets utility labels. The aim is a relaxed, expressive invitation. Risk: the visual play could distract from a quick decision.
