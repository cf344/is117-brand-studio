06 / Voice

Words are
design, too.

Casual, clear and friendly. Helpful without pressure.

Our voice in practice

“Start with what you already own.”

Short, direct, and specific about the next action.

Help first. Let visitors choose.

Offer three free outfit ideas before an optional preference. The ideas provide value (reciprocity); a freely chosen outfit direction leads to a matching suggestion and voluntary next step (consistency). Neither requires a purchase. No invented reviews, urgency or product facts.

A living reference. Change the shared tokens and logo, then compare this board with the storefront at the same width. Check the logo, typography, components, color roles, and voice text; sharing a stylesheet alone does not keep every example current. Save the reason for your choices in SPEC.
